Restoring the Details

About

When I started out as an Elvis Tribute Artist, professionally made jumpsuits were very expensive, so I had to turn to used suits. I found two on eBay that fit my budget, but they were in awful condition. With the investment I had already made, I had to work with what I had and make them stage-worthy.

For years, I restored only my own stagewear. As I shared photos of my work with fellow Elvis Tribute Artists, more and more performers began asking if I would restore their garments as well. That encouragement led to the creation of ETA Jumpsuit Restorations.

Since then, I've had the opportunity to work with garments from B&K Enterprises, AJM, ProElvis, and numerous independent jumpsuit makers. Along the way, I've built relationships with jumpsuit makers, manufacturers, and material suppliers throughout the industry, allowing me to source authentic materials and replacement components for accurate restorations.

Every restoration is approached with the same care, precision, and respect for the original craftsmanship of the garment.
