About

When I started out as an Elvis Tribute Artist, professionally made jumpsuits were out of budget, so I bought two used ones on eBay instead. Both arrived in awful condition — I had no choice but to learn to make them stage-worthy myself.

For years, I restored only my own stagewear. As I shared photos of my work with fellow Elvis Tribute Artists, more and more performers began asking if I would restore their garments as well. That encouragement led to the creation of ETA Jumpsuit Restorations.

Since then, I've worked on garments from B&K Enterprises, AJM, ProElvis, and numerous independent makers — and built relationships with manufacturers and material suppliers along the way, so I can source authentic parts for accurate repairs.

Every restoration is approached with the same care, precision, and respect for the original craftsmanship of the garment.
