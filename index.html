About

ETA Jumpsuit Restorations was founded from a simple need: repairing my own jumpsuits after purchasing two worn suits that needed far more work than expected. What began as restoring my own stagewear became years of learning the craftsmanship, materials, and construction techniques behind Elvis Tribute Artist clothing.

Today, I restore clothing for Elvis Tribute Artists throughout the United States and around the world. My work includes jumpsuits, capes, belts, jackets, shirts, pants, vests, and other stagewear, with every repair completed to preserve the original craftsmanship and appearance of each garment.

Over the years, I've completed more than 700 restorations for 150+ clients across 45+ U.S. states and 15+ countries. I've worked with garments from many of the industry's most respected makers, including B&K Enterprises, AJM, ProElvis, and numerous independent jumpsuit makers.

Those experiences have allowed me to build lasting relationships throughout the ETA community and with manufacturers and suppliers of the materials used to build and restore these garments. From rhinestones and rim settings to fabrics, linings, hardware, and specialty materials, I know where to source the components needed to complete restorations as accurately as possible.

Whether your stagewear needs a few missing stones replaced, a new lining, a zipper, alterations, or a complete restoration, every project receives the same level of care and attention to detail — so it's ready for the next performance.
